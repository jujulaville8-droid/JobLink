# Professional-context boundary: source-only review required

This supersedes the earlier canonical-skill-only provider boundary. The older paid fictional probes do not validate this expanded input. All runtime gates remain OFF. `EMPLOYER_SCREENING_CONTEXT_PRIVACY_APPROVED` is a separate prerequisite for new captures and provider dispatch; the previous privacy approval cannot implicitly authorize this boundary. No real applicant data has been processed or sent during implementation.

## Intended data flow

The original captured PDF and its SHA-256 remain immutable. A bounded local parser identifies candidate professional records and surrounding qualification context. Only admitted, complete source records can become contextual evidence. These records retain exact local page/spans; there is no quote reconstructed by removing an internal sensitive segment. Local document-presentation checks still operate independently, without a model.

The provider receives positional evidence/context IDs, finite job criteria/categories, bounded admitted professional text, qualification cautions and generic omission markers. Applicant/application/employer IDs, source hashes and page coordinates are not sent. Full pages, original PDF bytes and arbitrary unrestricted résumé fields are never fallback input. Source order is represented by derived positions rather than raw source coordinates.

A context record distinguishes an exact source statement from a fixed locally derived caution or generic omitted-context marker. A derived caution is not a verbatim quote. Context IDs cannot be cited as job evidence. The final employer report quotes only admitted original evidence slices selected through validated IDs; model-written quotes are not accepted.

## Privacy and interpretation are distinct

The bounded reviewed vocabulary gate accepts professional sentences assembled from reviewed words, rather than requiring an exact stock task phrase. Detected identity/contact/protected/instruction content or unsupported tokens cause the whole affected record to be omitted. The omission marker does not reveal a protected attribute or private rejection reason. Strong locally identified negative/third-party/training/invalid-credential qualifiers remain conservative cautions; they cannot be overridden by a positive model answer.

This is risk reduction, not proof of complete de-identification or semantic correctness. Names can coincide with ordinary words, professional vocabulary varies, and source reading order can be ambiguous. Unsupported language/terminology may reduce coverage. These limitations must be evaluated before activation, not hidden behind a claim of universal résumé understanding. Employment dates are not inherently protected attributes; current exclusion of raw dates is an engineering minimization choice, not a legal rule. This implementation makes no tenure, age, career-gap or licence-validity inference.

The model interprets admitted narrative and relevant context. Deterministic code validates schema, supplied evidence IDs/category, fixed disqualifier guards and arithmetic; it does not prove the truth or semantic meaning of arbitrary prose. Results are applicant-reported, AI-assessed and unverified. Missing, ambiguous or materially omitted support is unknown, never a negative applicant finding. Unrelated optional omissions do not mechanically erase all other professional evidence.

## Required approval and verification

Before any real-data activation, responsible owners must review exact outbound fixtures and residual privacy/coverage risks, approve OpenAI as the recipient for this minimized professional-context purpose, review the applicant notice/retention/legal basis, and complete the existing budget, storage, delivery and deployment prerequisites. Source publication alone is not this approval. No live privacy policy has been changed.

Offline tests cover source binding, whole-record exclusions, surrounding and cross-page qualifiers, ordinary hospitality/admin/trades narrative, malformed citations, invented context citations and known negative scope. Mocked semantic outputs do not establish model accuracy. The expanded boundary has not received a new live-provider evaluation; a future paid evaluation requires new explicit authorization and budget.
